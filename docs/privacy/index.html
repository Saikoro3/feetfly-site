Privacy / FEET FLY

Privacy, plainly stated.

FEET FLY is a small visual experiment. This page explains what the app and this site do with information.

Last updated · July 25, 2026

On this page App data Apple Maps Support email This website Changes

01 / App data

The app does not need to know where you are.

FEET FLY does not request device-location permission, require an account, run advertising, or use analytics SDKs. The selected place and the simulation settings are used to create the current experience on your device.

The app does not send selected places to a FEET FLY server, and it does not cache Apple Maps imagery. Network, map, and system services can still process information as part of providing their own features.

02 / Apple Maps

Maps are provided by Apple.

When you search for a place or load satellite imagery and 3D elevation, FEET FLY uses Apple MapKit. Apple may receive and process requests needed to provide those map features under its own terms and privacy policy.

Read Apple’s Privacy Policy ↗

03 / Support email

You choose what to send us.

If you email support, we receive the email address, message, and any attachments you decide to include. We use that information to respond to your request and diagnose the issue you describe.

Please do not send passwords, payment details, precise personal location information, or other sensitive information unless it is necessary and appropriate for your request.

Contact FEET FLY support →

04 / This website

No trackers hiding in the map lines.

This static site does not use cookies, advertising pixels, third-party analytics, or embedded external fonts. If you switch to Japanese, the site stores that language preference in your browser’s local storage so it can remember your choice. That preference is not sent to us.

05 / Changes

If this page changes, the date changes too.

We may update this page when the app, website, or support process changes. The date at the top of this page identifies the latest revision.
