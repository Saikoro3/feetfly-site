FEET FLY keeps the controls small so the descent can stay large.

The map is the stage

A place you recognize makes the distance feel real.

The scene uses Apple satellite imagery and available 3D elevation as context. The camera begins overhead, then eases toward the ground as the simulated altitude falls.

  • +Choose a city, address, landmark, or map point.
  • +Keep the map and the physics on the same altitude.

Under the hood

A simplified atmosphere, made visible.

The model is intentionally legible: enough structure to make the journey interesting, never a claim of prediction.

9.80665 m/s²

EDUCATIONAL SIMULATION

FEET FLY does not model a person, equipment, terrain, weather, or safe procedures. Its imagery is atmospheric context, not real-world guidance.
